Michael, Brittany, and the two boys — 7 and 4 — were back at San Diego
Comic-Con for 2026. A four-day badge Thursday through Sunday, with Saturday
kept clear as a rest day, so three days on the exhibit hall floor, the cosplay
along the marina, and easy evenings downtown by the bay.

## The Weekend

- **Thursday & Friday** — the exhibit hall floor, the booths, and the first
  walks of the show.
- **Saturday** — a rest day, off the floor.
- **Sunday** — back on the floor to close out the show.
- **Downtown** — the Gaslamp Quarter, offsite happenings, and dinners along the
  bay.
